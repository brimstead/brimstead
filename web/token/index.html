What $BRIM is for

Staking in BrimDrip. Stake $BRIM and receive ETH. Every wei of ETH that BrimDrip receives is streamed to stakers, pro rata to stake, linearly over 7 days. The ETH comes from:

  • the vault's protocol fee: 1% of each premium, taken when an offer settles (never on refunds);
  • Pons creator fees from $BRIM trading, which the team sends to BrimDrip by hand after launch.

A new stream starts once at least 0.005 ETH is queued; smaller amounts wait until they add up. Staking opens once the $BRIM address is set in BrimDrip, which can be done only once, right after launch. Using the vault does not require $BRIM. Any other use is to be announced.

No promised returns

$BRIM is not an investment product and nothing here is a promise of profit. The ETH stream depends entirely on vault usage and on the creator fees forwarded; it can be small or zero. The token price can fall to zero. Contracts: Independent review completed (no critical/high/medium findings); not a formal audit.

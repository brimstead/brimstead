Settle & claim

Anyone can settle an expired offer. The price used is the last Chainlink round at or before Friday 21:00 UTC; settling becomes possible once the next round has printed. If no valid close price can exist (feed paused or stale, or a corporate action at expiry), a refund opens 4 days after expiry, and unconditionally after 30 days.

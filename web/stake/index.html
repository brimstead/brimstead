How the drip works

  1. ETH arrives in BrimDrip: the vault's fee at each settlement, creator fees the team forwards, or anyone's payment.
  2. It waits in the queue until at least 0.005 ETH has gathered, so small payments do not keep pushing the end date back.
  3. Then a new 7-day stream starts: what is left of the current stream plus the queue, spread evenly. A payment that crosses the threshold starts it; otherwise anyone can press Start stream.
  4. Your share of the stream equals your share of all staked $BRIM while you are staked. While nobody is staked, nothing is given away: it goes back to the queue.
  5. Claim whenever you like. Unstake at any time; rewards earned stay claimable.

01

Write a call

Lock stock tokens, choose a strike above the live Chainlink price and a Friday expiry, set the premium per token in ETH.

Explore → 02

Buy upside

Pay the premium in ETH for the move above the strike at expiry. Only calls still below the strike can be bought, until 2 hours before expiry.

Explore → 03

Settle

Anyone can settle at the Friday 21:00 UTC close price once the next Chainlink round prints. If no valid close can exist, both sides are refunded in full.

Explore → 04

Claim

Buyers claim their stock above the strike, writers claim the rest of their tokens and the premium minus the 1% fee.
